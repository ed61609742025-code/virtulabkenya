SECTION I: CURRICULUM SCOPE

KNEC Paper 3 Examination Modules

VirtuLab Kenya covers every essential practical competency outlined in the Kenya Institute of Curriculum Development (KICD) and KNEC secondary syllabus.

MODULE 01 • VOLUMETRIC ANALYSIS

Acid-Base & Redox Titrations

Master pipetting, meniscus alignment, phenolphthalein/methyl orange transitions, and standardized redox titrations (KMnO₄ vs Fe²⁺ / Ethanedioic Acid).

C₁V₁ = C₂V₂ Redox (KMnO₄) Precipitation Titres

MODULE 02 • INORGANIC ANALYSIS

Qualitative Salt Deductions

Systematic aqueous testing for Cations (Pb²⁺, Al³⁺, Fe²⁺, Fe³⁺, Cu²⁺, Zn²⁺, Ca²⁺, Mg²⁺) and Anions (SO₄²⁻, Cl⁻, CO₃²⁻, SO₃²⁻, NO₃⁻) using NaOH and NH₃(aq).

Cation Confirmation White/Coloured Ppt Excess Solubility
Launch Salt Testing Bench →
MODULE 03 • PHYSICAL CHEMISTRY

Reaction Rates & Kinetics

Investigate the disappearing cross method (Na₂S₂O₃ + HCl), temperature coefficients, activation energy gradients, and 1/t rate curves.

Disappearing Cross Rate Gradients 1/Time Graphs
Launch Reaction Rates Rig →
MODULE 04 • THERMOCHEMISTRY

Energy Changes & Enthalpy

Calorimetric measurement of Enthalpy of Neutralization (ΔH_neut), Solution (ΔH_soln), and Metal Displacement with temperature-time extrapolation.

ΔH = mcΔT / n Calorimeter Cooling Exo/Endothermic
Launch Calorimetry Lab →
MODULE 05 • PHYSICAL CHEMISTRY

Solubility & Crystallization

Determine crystallization temperatures across varying solute masses (KClO₃ / KNO₃) and plot official solubility curves (g solute per 100g water).

Solubility Curves Crystal Formation Fractional Sep.
Launch Solubility Bench →
MODULE 06 • ORGANIC QUALITATIVE

Organic Functional Groups

Test for unsaturation (C=C / C≡C) using acidified KMnO₄ and Bromine water; confirm Alkanols and Carboxylic Acids through esterification and sodium tests.

Unsaturation Test Esterification Litmus / NaHCO₃

Gas Preparation & Collection

Generate, dry, and collect common laboratory gases (O₂, CO₂, Cl₂, NH₃, SO₂, H₂) with authentic thistle funnels, drying agents, and confirmatory tests.
