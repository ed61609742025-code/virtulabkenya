💡 KNEC Chemistry Paper 3 Organic Analysis Tips

❌ Common Penalty Traps:
  • Do NOT write "turns clear" for Bromine water: Write "bromine water is decolourized" or "turns colourless".
  • Do NOT write "smoke produced": Write "burns with a yellow sooty / smoky flame".
  • Do NOT deduce "Alkane" when testing with K₂Cr₂O₇: Deduce "R-OH / Primary or secondary alkanol absent".
✅ Marking Best Practices:
  • Unsaturation: Write "-C=C- or -C≡C- present" when bromine water is decolourized.
  • Oxidation with Acidified K₂Cr₂O₇: Orange solution turning emerald green indicates "R-OH / Alkanol present".
  • Carbonate Effervescence: Effervescence of a gas turning limewater milky indicates "R-COOH / Carboxylic acid / H⁺ ions present".
