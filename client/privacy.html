STATUTORY PRIVACY GOVERNANCE

Privacy Policy & Data Protection Protocol

Effective Date: 1st September 2026 • Jurisdiction: Republic of Kenya (KDPA 2019) • Version 1.0
✨ Privacy First Commitment: VirtuLab Kenya is an educational science simulator engineered with an offline-first architecture. Practical simulations run locally on your device storage with zero commercial tracking, zero profiling of minor learners, and zero data sharing with third-party advertisers.

1. Introduction & Identity of Data Controller

VirtuLab Kenya ("VirtuLab", "we", "our", or "the Platform") operates an authoritative virtual secondary school chemistry practical simulation and diagnostic assessment platform. This Privacy Policy sets out how we collect, store, utilize, and safeguard the personal data of secondary school learners, science instructors, and institutional administrators in full compliance with the Data Protection Act No. 24 of 2019 (Laws of Kenya) and the regulations established by the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC).

VirtuLab Kenya acts as the Data Controller for self-registered learners and visitors, and acts as a Data Processor on behalf of registered secondary schools and science departments that utilize the Platform for classroom instruction and assessment.

2. Protection of Minor Learners' Data (Section 33, KDPA 2019)

⚠️ Special Protection for Secondary School Learners: Most secondary school candidates in Kenya (Forms 1 to 4) are minors under the age of 18 years. In strict compliance with Section 33 of the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019 and the Children Act 2022, processing a minor's data requires lawful authorization and heightened safeguards.

We implement the following statutory safeguards for minors:

  • Institutional Proxy Consent (*In Loco Parentis*): When a student is enrolled through their secondary school or science teacher using an authorized School Registration Code, the institution certifies that it acts *in loco parentis* and possesses statutory consent from parents/guardians to administer educational coursework.
  • Self-Registration Consent: Independent learners registering on the platform must confirm that they have attained 18 years of age or have received direct authorization from a parent, legal guardian, or chemistry instructor.
  • Prohibition of Commercial Exploitation: We strictly prohibit any commercial exploitation, behavioral targeted advertising, or algorithmic credit scoring of minor learners.
  • Data Minimization: We strictly refuse to collect national identification numbers (ID), phone numbers, or residential GPS addresses from student accounts.

3. Categories of Personal Data We Collect

We collect only the minimum data required to facilitate virtual chemistry simulations and academic progress tracking:

  • Account Identity: Candidate full name, school email address, academic class level (Form 1, 2, 3, or 4), and school registration code.
  • Authentication Data: Cryptographically hashed passwords (salted using industry-standard bcrypt). VirtuLab staff never have access to plain-text student passwords.
  • Laboratory Practical Telemetry: Titration trial values, volumetric meniscus readings, salt analysis deduction entries, kinetic experiment reaction times, mock exam scores, and competency badge attainments.
  • Technical Offline Data: Service worker cache records, IndexedDB local storage states, and local device theme preferences (`vlk_theme`).

4. Offline-First Architecture & Local Storage

VirtuLab Kenya operates as an Offline-First Progressive Web App (PWA). All chemical simulation mathematics, apparatus rendering, and trial calculations are processed locally inside your browser's client sandbox. Your practical trial drafts remain saved locally in your device's browser memory (LocalStorage / IndexedDB) and are transmitted to the cloud only when you explicitly submit an assignment or when connectivity is restored.

5. Lawful Basis & Purpose of Processing

We process personal data under Section 30 of the KDPA 2019 based on:

  1. Contractual & Educational Performance: Delivering interactive KNEC Paper 3 simulation modules and computing assessment results.
  2. Legitimate Educational Interest: Enabling science teachers and secondary schools to identify practical learning deficiencies, review volumetric concordancy errors, and dispatch personalized revision feedback.
  3. Explicit Consent: Provided upon student account creation or school onboarding.

6. Data Sharing & Third Parties

VirtuLab Kenya does not sell, rent, monetize, or trade student personal data with third parties. Data is disclosed only in the following strictly limited contexts:

  • Authorized Teachers & Science Departments: A student's practical performance ledger, mock examination booklets, and speed battle scores are visible only to the verified teacher whose classroom code is linked to the student.
  • Secure Cloud Infrastructure: Encrypted database hosting with strict role-based access control (RBAC) and end-to-end TLS/HTTPS transmission encryption.
  • Statutory Legal Requests: When mandated by a lawful order issued by a court of competent jurisdiction within the Republic of Kenya.

7. Rights of Data Subjects (Section 26, KDPA 2019)

Learners, parents, and educators enjoy full statutory rights under the Kenya Data Protection Act, including:

  • Right of Access: Request a complete digital copy of your academic ledger and stored profile data.
  • Right to Rectification: Update or correct erroneous candidate information through your workstation settings.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request permanent deletion of student accounts and associated examination attempts upon completion of secondary education.
  • Right to Data Portability: Export your practical performance records in standardized CSV format.

8. Data Security Measures

We enforce rigorous technical and administrative security controls, including automated API rate limiting, TLS 1.3 cryptographic transport security, HTTP security headers (Content Security Policy, X-Frame-Options), and strict database perimeter firewalls.
