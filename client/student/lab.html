KNEC 233/3 Senior Examiner Guidelines — Volumetric Analysis (Q1)

Critical Errors that Forfeit Table 1 Marks
  • Inconsistent Decimal Places: All burette readings (initial, final, and titre) must be recorded consistently to 1 or 2 decimal places (e.g., 0.0 or 0.00). Inconsistency forfeits the decimal accuracy mark (-1.0 Mk).
  • Averaging Non-Concordant Values: Concordant titres must be within ±0.20 cm³ of each other (±0.10 cm³ is ideal). Averaging values differing by more than ±0.20 cm³ incurs an automatic penalty (-1.0 Mk).
  • Unticked Concordant Titres: You must tick (✓) the specific trials used to compute the average titre in Table 1.
  • Arithmetic Subtraction Inaccuracy: Ensure Final Volume - Initial Volume = Titre is calculated with exact mathematical accuracy (-1.0 Mk for arithmetic slip).
Calculations & Stoichiometry Mark Rules
  • Formula Working: Always show step-by-step substitution: Moles = (Molarity × Average Volume) / 1000.
  • Mole Ratio Application: Explicitly write out the balanced stoichiometric reaction equation and state the mole ratio (e.g. 1:1, 1:2, or 2:1) before computing moles of the reactant in the conical flask.
  • Rounding & Significant Figures: Retain at least 4 decimal places for moles during working (e.g. 0.00245 mol) to avoid premature rounding error in the final molarity / mass concentration.
  • Mandatory Units: State units clearly on all final answers: mol/dm³ (or M) for molarity, g/dm³ for mass concentration, and g/mol for relative formula mass (RFM).
