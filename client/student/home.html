🧪 Cation Deduction Reference

Cu²⁺ (Copper): Blue ppt with NaOH or Ammonia. Soluble in excess NH₃(aq) giving a deep royal blue solution.
Fe²⁺ (Iron II): Dirty green precipitate with NaOH/Ammonia, insoluble in excess.
Fe³⁺ (Iron III): Rust brown precipitate with NaOH/Ammonia, insoluble in excess.
Pb²⁺, Zn²⁺, Al³⁺: White ppt with NaOH, soluble in excess. Only Zn²⁺ dissolves in excess Ammonia.
