⚠️ Chemical Safety & SDS

1. Sodium Thiosulfate (Na₂S₂O₃): Low acute toxicity. Reaction with HCl produces sulfur precipitate and sulfur dioxide gas.

2. Hydrochloric Acid (HCl 2.0M): Corrosive. Causes skin and eye irritation. In virtual lab, handled safely without chemical contact.

3. Sulfur Dioxide (SO₂ Gas Hazard): Choking, pungent gas released in the disappearing cross practical. In physical labs, rooms must be well ventilated. In VirtuLab, zero environmental or health hazard.

4. Hydrogen Peroxide (H₂O₂ 20 vol): Strong oxidizer. Rapid exothermic decomposition with MnO₂ catalyst producing oxygen gas.
