n = (Molarity × Volume) / 1000 or n = mass / RAM

KNEC Examiner Marking Rubric

Paper 3 Thermochemistry Practical Advice
1. Table Marks (5.0 Marks Total):
• Complete Table (1m): All required readings recorded.
• Decimals Consistency (1m): Temperature readings given consistently to 1 decimal place (e.g. 22.0°C or 22.5°C).
• Mixing Point (1m): NO reading recorded at mixing time (t = 2.5 min; indicated by hyphen or 'X').
• Accuracy (2m): Temperature change ΔT within ±1.5°C of supervisor's value.
2. Graph Marks (5.0 Marks Total):
• Scale & Axes (2m): Uniform intervals covering > 50% grid, correctly labeled axes with units.
• Plotting (1m): Accurate plotting of points with small 'x'.
• Cooling Extrapolation (2m): Straight line drawn through cooling points and extended back to mixing time ($t = 2.5\text{ min}$) to give true maximum temperature $T_{\max}$.
3. Calculations (5.0 Marks Total):
• Heat Q: $Q = m \cdot c \cdot \Delta T$ using correct total mass ($1.0\text{ g/cm}^3$) and $c = 4.2\text{ J/g/}^\circ\text{C}$.
• Moles n: Moles of limiting reagent correctly determined.
• Molar Enthalpy ΔH: Correct sign (negative for exothermic, positive for endothermic) and correct units ($\text{kJ/mol}$).
