⚠️ Critical KNEC Examination Precautions

  • Thistle Funnel Safety Seal: Stem must dip below the liquid surface to prevent evolved gas from escaping into the lab room.
  • Airtight Joints: All rubber bungs and delivery tube joints must be securely sealed to maintain pressure.
  • Collection Logic: Over water is optimal for insoluble gases; upward displacement of air for gases denser than air.

🧪 Chemical Equation & State Symbols

2H₂O₂(aq) —[MnO₂]→ 2H₂O(l) + O₂(g)

Drying Compatibility: Neutral and acidic gases can be dried using concentrated H₂SO₄ or fused CaCl₂. Basic gases (NH₃) react vigorously with acid and must only be dried with Quicklime (CaO).
