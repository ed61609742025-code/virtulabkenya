## 1. Executive Summary & Overview

Setting a secondary chemistry practical examination (KCSE Paper 3) is traditionally one of the most demanding tasks for science teachers in Kenya. A teacher must:
1. Balance chemical reaction stoichiometry to yield realistic, concordant titre volumes between $15.00\text{ cm}^3$ and $30.00\text{ cm}^3$.
2. Formulate diagnostic qualitative inorganic analysis test sequences that systematically confirm cations ($Pb^{2+}, Cu^{2+}, Fe^{2+}, Fe^{3+}, Al^{3+}, Zn^{2+}, Ca^{2+}, NH_4^+$) and anions ($SO_4^{2-}, SO_3^{2-}, CO_3^{2-}, Cl^-, NO_3^-$).
3. Design organic functional group testing sequences that cleanly differentiate alkanols, carboxylic acids, and unsaturated hydrocarbons.
4. Prepare official KNEC scoring schemes and write the **Confidential Instructions to Laboratory Technicians** detailing exact reagent recipes and apparatus requirements per candidate.

**Walimu AI Exam Assistant** automates this entire workflow in seconds. Teachers can either upload an existing paper (PDF, photo scan, or text) or simply type their curriculum idea. The assistant generates the full exam, marking scheme, technician guide, and wires the parameters directly into the VirtuLab Kenya student virtual laboratory!

## 2. Dual-Mode Creation Workflows

### Mode A: Uploading Past Exam Papers (Multimodal Extraction)

Teachers often have physical printouts of past district mock papers (e.g. Nairobi Joint Mock, Mang'u Invitational, Alliance High School Form 4 Mock, or past KCSE papers). 

1. Navigate to **"🤖 AI Exam Assistant"** in the Teacher Console sidebar.
2. Select the **"📄 Upload Exam Paper"** mode pill.
3. Drag and drop your file or click the upload zone:
   - **Supported file types**: PDF (`.pdf`), high-resolution camera photos (`.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.png`, `.webp`), and plain text (`.txt`).
   - **Maximum file size**: 20MB.
4. *(Optional)* Enter any teacher-specific constraints in the **Teacher Notes** field (e.g. *"Focus on Question 1 and Question 2"* or *"Adjust titration concentration to 0.1M"*).
5. Click **"⚡ Analyze Paper & Set Exam"**.
6. The multimodal vision engine reads the questions, extracts the reagents, concentrations, and test tables, and constructs an executable simulation blueprint.

### Mode B: Idea to Exam (Pedagogical Prompting)

When a teacher does not have a physical paper but wants an exam tailored to their current term scheme of work:

1. Select the **"💡 From an Idea or Syllabus Topic"** mode pill.
2. In the description box, express your exam goal. Examples:
   - *"Form 4 Term 2 mock exam with Redox titration of KMnO4 and Mohr's salt, an unknown zinc sulfate salt, and an organic test for an alkene with bromine water."*
   - *"Form 3 practical testing enthalpy of neutralization of HCl with NaOH and qualitative tests for chloride salts."*
   - *"A foundational revision test for students who need practice with concordant titres and amphoteric cations (Al3+ and Pb2+)."*
3. Select your options:
   - **Form Level**: Form 1, Form 2, Form 3, or Form 4.
   - **Exam Format**:
     - 🏆 *KCSE Composite Mock (40 Marks)* — 3 questions (Volumetric 15M, Qualitative 15M, Organic 10M).
     - 🧪 *Volumetric Analysis (15 Marks)* — Acid-Base, Redox, Precipitation, or Complexometric.
     - 🧂 *Qualitative Salt Analysis (15 Marks)* — Inorganic cation & anion identification.
     - ⚗️ *Organic Chemistry Lab (10 Marks)* — Functional group tests.
     - 🔥 *Thermochemistry / Energy Changes (15 Marks)* — Enthalpies of solution, neutralization, or displacement.
     - ⚡ *Reaction Rates & Kinetics (15 Marks)* — Disappearing cross and gas collection.
     - 💨 *Gas Preparation & Collection (10 Marks)* — Apparatus setup and confirmatory testing.
   - **Difficulty Standard**:
     - *KNEC National Standard* (standard KCSE level).
     - *Foundational Revision* (more guiding observations for revision).
     - *National School Challenge* (complex stoichiometry, multi-ion mixtures).
   - **Allocated Duration**: 30 to 180 minutes (default: 135 minutes).
4. Click **"🚀 Generate Exam with AI"**.

#### Instant National Curriculum Templates
For maximum convenience, click any of the 6 instant preset chips:
- 🧪 **Neutralization Classic** ($HCl + NaOH$, $Pb(NO_3)_2$, Ethanol)
- ⚡ **Redox Practical** ($KMnO_4 + Fe^{2+}$, $ZnSO_4$, Cyclohexene)
- 🔥 **Thermochemistry** (Enthalpy of neutralization with copper/carbonate)
- ⏱️ **Reaction Rates** (Disappearing cross with $Na_2S_2O_3$ and $HCl$)
- 💨 **Gas Preparation** ($CO_2$ synthesis, drying, and lime water test)
- 🧂 **Two-Salt Separation** (Advanced Form 4 separation of $BaSO_4$ and $ZnSO_4$)

## 3. The 4-Perspective Interactive Reviewer

Once generated, the exam is presented across four interactive sub-tabs:

### Tab 1: ⚙️ Simulation Blueprint
Displays the digital laboratory bench parameters:
- **Question 1 Volumetric Setup**: Burette reagent, flask sample, pipette volume ($25.0\text{ cm}^3$ or $20.0\text{ cm}^3$), indicator (phenolphthalein, methyl orange, screened methyl orange, starch), stoichiometric ratio, and calculated theoretical titre.
- **Question 2 Qualitative Setup**: Unknown sample name (e.g. Solid Y), confirmed cation and anion, physical appearance, and the complete step-by-step diagnostic reagent procedure.
- **Question 3 Organic Setup**: Liquid/Solid sample name, confirmed functional group, and testing reagents.

### Tab 2: 📜 Student Question Paper
Renders a clean, formatted KNEC examination paper ready for printing or student preview, including:
- Official paper title, timing, and general instructions.
- Titration Table 1 with trial columns (I, II, III), initial/final burette readings, and volume used.
- Structured qualitative observation and inference tables.
- Calculation sub-questions with allocated marks.

### Tab 3: 🎯 KNEC Marking Scheme
Provides teachers with an authoritative marking guide:
- Complete breakdown of marks per question.
- Strict KNEC concordancy rules ($\pm 0.10\text{ cm}^3$ or $\pm 0.20\text{ cm}^3$).
- Mandatory keywords required for observation and inference marks (e.g. *"white precipitate soluble in excess"*, *"effervescence of a colorless gas"*).
- Calculation formulas and intermediate substitution marks.

### Tab 4: 🧪 Lab Technician Confidential Instructions
Detailed laboratory preparation guide for the school science technician:
- Exact recipes to prepare solutions (e.g. *"Dissolve 3.16 g of KMnO₄ in 1 dm³ of distilled water containing 50 cm³ of concentrated H₂SO₄"*).
- Quantities required per candidate ($150\text{ cm}^3$ of Solution A, $2.0\text{ g}$ of Solid Y, etc.).
- Complete apparatus checklist (burettes, pipettes, test tubes, racks, Bunsen burners).

## 4. Conversational Refinement ("Walimu AI Co-Pilot")

Secondary teachers often need to make quick tweaks based on available laboratory apparatus or lesson goals. The bottom **Walimu AI Co-Pilot** drawer allows natural-language modifications:

- *"Change the pipette to 20 cm³"*
- *"Use screened methyl orange instead of phenolphthalein"*
- *"Change the unknown salt from Lead Nitrate to Zinc Sulfate"*
- *"Make the titration a dibasic acid (H₂SO₄) with mole ratio 1:2"*
- *"Add a specific caution about wearing safety goggles during the flame test"*

Click **"Refine ⚡"** and the assistant will update all calculations, observation tables, marking rubrics, and technician instructions simultaneously while preserving chemical validity.

## 5. Publishing to Class & Student Execution

1. Click the green **"🚀 Publish Exam to Class"** button in the top action bar.
2. VirtuLab Kenya automatically:
   - Creates a formal assignment record in the platform database.
   - Sets a due date (default: 7 days).
   - Attaches the complete `exam_config` payload.
   - Dispatches real-time alerts to all students enrolled under your teacher account.
3. **Student Experience**:
   - Students receive an instant notification in their dashboard: *"New Exam: [Exam Title] has been assigned."*
   - Clicking the link opens `composite_exam.html?assignment=<id>`.
   - The virtual workbench dynamically loads the **exact Solutions A & B**, pipette volume, indicator color transitions, unknown salt precipitate reactions, and organic test reagents defined by the teacher!

## 6. Offline Resilience & Reliability

In rural Kenyan schools or computer laboratories with intermittent internet connectivity:
- The system includes verified offline KNEC mock series presets.
- If the Gemini AI service is temporarily unreachable or `GEMINI_API_KEY` is not yet configured, Walimu AI seamlessly falls back to a verified national mock layout, ensuring the teacher's lesson is never disrupted.
- All exams can be saved locally as JSON blueprints (`📋 Export JSON`) or printed to paper (`🖨️ Print Booklet`).
