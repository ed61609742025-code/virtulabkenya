🧪 KNEC Salt ID Reference

Cu²⁺ (Copper): Blue precipitate with NaOH or Ammonia. Dissolves in excess Ammonia to form **deep blue solution**.
Fe²⁺ (Iron II): **Dirty green** precipitate with NaOH or Ammonia, insoluble in excess. Turns brown at surface.
Fe³⁺ (Iron III): **Rust brown** precipitate with NaOH or Ammonia, insoluble in excess.
Pb²⁺, Zn²⁺, Al³⁺: **White** precipitate with NaOH, dissolves in excess to form colorless solutions. Zinc also dissolves in excess Ammonia.
