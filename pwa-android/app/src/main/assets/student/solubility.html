KNEC Examiner Marking Rubric

Paper 3 Solubility Practical Advice
1. Table Marks (5.0 Marks Total):
• Complete Table (1m): All 4–5 trials recorded.
• Decimals Consistency (1m): Temperature readings given consistently to 1 decimal place (e.g. 64.0°C).
• Accuracy (2m): Temperature readings within ±2.0°C of theoretical value.
• Trend (1m): Decreasing crystallization temperature with increasing water volume.
2. Graph Marks (3.0 Marks Total):
• Scale (1m): Occupies at least half of the grid in both directions with simple uniform intervals.
• Plotting (1m): Correct 'x' marks plotted accurately.
• Curve (1m): Smooth continuous best-fit curve drawn through points (do NOT use a ruler).
3. Common Student Mistakes:
• Overheating leading to water evaporation and higher apparent solubility.
• Failure to stir while cooling, causing supercooling where crystallization is delayed by 1–3°C.
