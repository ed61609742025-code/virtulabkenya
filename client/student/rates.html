Laboratory Safety Data Sheet (SDS)

• Hydrochloric Acid (HCl 2.0M): Corrosive. Causes burns and eye damage. Wear splash goggles. In case of skin contact, rinse with copious cold water.
• Sulfur Dioxide (SO₂ Gas): Released during thiosulfate precipitation (Na₂S₂O₃ + 2HCl → 2NaCl + SO₂ + S + H₂O). Pungent and chokes asthmatics. Keep room ventilated. Dispose into sodium carbonate wash bowl immediately after timing.
• Hydrogen Peroxide (H₂O₂ 20 Vol): Strong oxidizer and skin irritant. Causes white blanching of skin. Handle with dropper.
• Finely Divided Powders: Flammable and respiratory irritants. Avoid aerosolizing calcium carbonate powder.

KNEC Question 2 Practical Guidelines

1. Timing Accuracy (Stopwatch): The stopwatch must be started simultaneously as the acid hits the thiosulfate solution, with constant steady swirl. Record time $t$ to nearest second or 1 decimal place.
2. Reciprocal Calculation ($1/t$): Record $1/t$ consistently to 3 decimal places (e.g., 0.025, 0.040). An extra or missing decimal point loses the accuracy mark in KNEC Table 1.
3. SAPC Graph Rubric (4.0 Marks):
  • S (Scale): Plot covers more than 50% of the grid without awkward intervals like 3, 7, 9.
  • A (Axes): Both axes correctly labeled with units (e.g. $1/t$ ($s^{-1}$) on y-axis, Volume ($cm^3$) on x-axis).
  • P (Plotting): All experimental points accurately plotted to within $pm 0.5$ small square.
  • C (Curve / Best-Fit Line): Smooth curve or straight line passing through the origin $(0,0)$ with balanced distribution of points.
4. Tangent Construction ($dy/dx$): When asked for the instantaneous rate at time $t$, draw a neat tangent at that point, construct a right-angled gradient triangle with $Delta y$ and $Delta x ge 4 ext{cm}$, and evaluate with correct units.
