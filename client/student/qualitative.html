💡 KNEC Chemistry Paper 3 Inorganic Salt Analysis Tips

Use this guide to avoid common mark penalties in KNEC salt analysis examinations:

❌ Common Penalty Traps:
  • Do NOT write "white solution": Write "white precipitate" or "colourless solution".
  • Do NOT write "gas produced": State observations (e.g. "effervescence of a colourless gas that turns moist litmus blue").
  • Do NOT forget excess reagent observation: Always state whether the precipitate is "soluble in excess" or "insoluble in excess".
✅ Marking Best Practices:
  • Cation Deductions: List ionic charges (e.g. Zn²⁺, Al³⁺, Pb²⁺ present for white precipitate soluble in excess NaOH).
  • Confirmatory Tests: Mention specific ions (e.g. Cu²⁺ present for deep blue solution in excess NH₃).
  • Anion Deductions: Write exact ionic formulas (e.g. SO₄²⁻ present for white precipitate insoluble in dilute HNO₃).
