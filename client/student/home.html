KNEC MARKING TRAP Tip 1/12

Burette Reading Precision (Table 1)

KNEC Paper 3 penalizes any titre recorded to only 1 decimal place. Always record initial & final readings to 2 decimal places ending strictly in .00 or .50 (e.g. 24.50 cm³, not 24.5 cm³).
